# Proposals & Outcomes

Atlas normalizes proposals from multiple governance systems while preserving links to the original source. Proposal pages can show lifecycle status, voting window, choices, quorum context, outcome, discussion links, and available vote data.

Lifecycle and outcome answer different questions: lifecycle describes where the proposal is in its process, while outcome describes the result when known. An active proposal normally has no final outcome. Always open the official source before acting on execution-sensitive information.
