# Votes & Voters

Proposal vote views summarize choice distribution, turnout, voting power, quorum, and available ballot rows. Coverage indicators matter: a visible proposal can still have unavailable or partial vote data.

DAO voter rankings describe observed participation within that DAO. Voter profiles provide cross-DAO history when supported. Rankings are research aids, not identity, reputation, or delegation recommendations; voting power can reflect delegation and source-specific rules.
