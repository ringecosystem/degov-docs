# Discussions, Timelines & Signals

When forum coverage exists, Atlas links governance discussions with the DAO context around them. Timelines aggregate proposal or voting activity over time. Governance feeds show recent source events, while priority signals highlight items that may deserve faster attention.

Signals are navigation and research aids, not security verdicts. Read the linked proposal, forum thread, and executable actions before making a governance decision.
